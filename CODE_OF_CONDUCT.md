# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), in full. The linked text is the governing policy; the following is a short guide.

Treat everyone respectfully. Welcome different backgrounds, accept constructive feedback, and take responsibility for mistakes. Harassment, sexualized conduct, personal attacks, and publishing private information without permission are unacceptable.

The policy applies in project spaces and when representing the community elsewhere. The maintainer, [@66Hamidreza](https://github.com/66Hamidreza), enforces it and may remove contributions or restrict participation. Consequences follow the Covenant's ladder: correction, warning, temporary ban, and permanent ban, depending on impact and repetition.

To report an incident, use GitHub's **Report content** control on the relevant comment, issue, or discussion; include the context and identify the content involved. Reports handled by GitHub are subject to GitHub's reporting process. For visible project violations, mention the maintainer without repeating abusive or private material. No separate private maintainer contact is currently published; do not post sensitive incident details publicly. Community leaders must protect reporters' privacy and review incidents fairly.

Attribution: Contributor Covenant 2.1; its enforcement guidelines draw on Mozilla's enforcement ladder. See the [Covenant FAQ](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/faq/) and [translations](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/translations/).
